Ying Xu

I'm a software engineer at Pine59 in Oslo, building location intelligence that helps businesses understand how places and populations change. Before that, I did my PhD in deepfake detection at Colourlab, Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU).

My thesis, Generalising Deepfake Detection Towards Robust, Interpretable and Fair Models, looks at how detectors generalize to unseen manipulations, whether they are fair across demographic groups, and whether benchmarks really measure what they claim to. I was advised by Kiran Raja, Marius Pedersen and Luisa Verdoliva.
